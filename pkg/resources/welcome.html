Driver for the HP LaserJet P1005

This installs a driver that lets current macOS print to the HP LaserJet P1005 over USB. It runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 12 or later and does not need Rosetta or HP's old installer.

  • The printer needs HP's firmware, which is not part of the public package. If it is missing, the installer downloads HP's official driver package (584 MB) from Apple, takes the firmware out of it and deletes the rest. This needs an internet connection and about 600 MB of free space for a moment.
  • Plug in and switch on the printer before installing to have it set up automatically. You can also add it later in System Settings > Printers & Scanners.
  • The first page after switching the printer on takes a few seconds longer, because the firmware is sent first.

This is a hobby project, not made or supported by HP or Apple, and it comes without any warranty.

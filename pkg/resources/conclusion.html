The driver is installed

  • Print from any app with Cmd+P and choose HP LaserJet P1005. Resolution, print density and EconoMode are under Printer Options.
  • If the printer is not in the list yet, connect it, switch it on and add it in System Settings > Printers & Scanners > Add Printer. This driver is selected automatically.
  • To remove the driver, run this in Terminal:
    sudo /Library/Printers/p1005-native/uninstall.sh

More information: github.com/archie1602/p1005-mac-driver
